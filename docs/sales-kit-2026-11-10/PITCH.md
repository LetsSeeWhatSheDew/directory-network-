# PuffPrice — how to sell it (Nov 10, 2026)

You're selling one thing: the **Market Report**.

- $99 a month, founding price for the first five stores.
- The first report is free.
- Billed by invoice, paid by ACH or check.
- Listing stays free. Nobody can buy rank.

A printed report for every one of the 26 stores is in `market-reports/`. Bring the store's own report and hand it over. The report does the selling.

## The 30-second version

> "I run PuffPrice, a free deal finder for Central Illinois. Every morning it reads every dispensary's own website and menu, 26 stores in 9 cities, and shows shoppers the best deal near them. Nobody pays to rank, including you.
>
> I made you something. This is how your deals and shelf prices stacked up against every store within 15 miles this month. Keep it, it's free. If it's useful, I'll send you a new one every month for $99."

Then stop talking and let them read page 1.

## On your phone (the demo)

1. Open **puffprice.com/dispensary/&lt;their-slug&gt;**. "This is what shoppers see for you right now." The deal count is real now: duplicates were removed Nov 10.
2. Open **puffprice.com/city/&lt;their-city&gt;**. "This is the page Google is showing people." Peoria's city page appeared 453 times in 4 weeks.
3. Open **puffprice.com/for-dispensaries/&lt;their-slug&gt;/market-report**. That's the live version of the paper report.

## What to point at in their report

## Objections

| They say | You say |
| --- | --- |
| "Can I pay to be on top?" | "No. Not you, not anyone. That's why shoppers trust it, and why it's worth being on." |
| "We already use Weedmaps / Leafly." | "They rank by who pays. I rank by the deal. I also read your own site, so shoppers see what you actually posted." |
| "Where do you get our deals? Did we agree to this?" | "Only what you publish for shoppers: your own site and official social. Every deal links back to you. Want something fixed or taken down? Same day." |
| "How many people use it?" | Be straight: "It's early. Google showed PuffPrice about 3,400 times in the last four weeks, more than double the month before. The report isn't an ad. It's what your competitors are doing." **Don't quote Google Analytics' "+536%". That was mostly bots.** |
| "$99 is a lot." | "The first one's free. If the second isn't worth $99, don't buy the third." |
| "Send me something." | Leave the printed report plus `ONE-PAGER.pdf`. Write their slug on it. |
| "Do you take card?" | "Invoice, then ACH or check." (Card processors exclude cannabis-related businesses. Don't promise card.) |

## Don't say

- Any traffic number that isn't in `TRACTION.md`.
- "Partner", "featured" or "sponsored". There's nothing to buy except the report.
- Anything about Pro for shoppers. It isn't for sale until a payment processor says yes in writing.
- Anything that sounds like a consumption pitch. This is a business tool for a licensed retailer.
