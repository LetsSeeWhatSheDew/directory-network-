## Payments

- **Stripe:** businesses where more than 25% of services target marijuana sellers are supported only outside the US.
- **Square, PayPal/Braintree:** refuse marijuana-related businesses.
- **Bluevine, Relay:** refuse cannabis and ancillary businesses.
- **Mercury:** reportedly refuses (UNVERIFIED).
- **Lemon Squeezy:** says to ask first. **Paddle:** unknown.
- **High-risk processors** (PaymentCloud, Durango, Paybotic): take cannabis-related merchants at roughly 3–8% plus reserves.
- **Banks:** Credit Union 1 runs an Illinois cannabis program with Shield Compliance. Illinois law protects state banks and credit unions that serve cannabis-related businesses.

**Path:**
1. An Illinois credit union or community bank account, disclosed honestly as media and data for dispensaries.
2. Plain invoices paid by ACH for the Market Report.
3. No consumer card subscription for now.
